# App.Mcp Expense Policy
 
Version 4.1, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all employees and contractors.
 
## 1. General rules
 
1. Claim in the currency you actually paid in. Finance converts to GBP using the published
   reference rate for the date the expense was incurred, not the date you filed the report.
2. Submit a report within 30 days of the earliest expense on it. Reports submitted after
   30 days need written approval from a director.
3. Attach a receipt for every line over 75.00 GBP, or the equivalent in the currency claimed.
4. One report per trip or per calendar month. Do not mix a trip with routine monthly costs.
 
## 3. Accommodation
 
1. The limit is 180.00 GBP per night including tax, for all locations.
2. London, Zurich, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo and Singapore carry a higher limit of
   260.00 GBP per night.
 
## 4. Meals
 
1. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60.00 GBP per person per day.
4. Any meal line over 150.00 GBP must be itemised per person.
 
## 6. Communications and equipment
 
2. Home broadband is not reimbursable. It is covered by the monthly remote working allowance
   and must not appear on an expense report.
